Pelek Properties Real Estate and Booking Website

Overview

Pelek Properties is a real estate and hospitality company offering Airbnb accommodation, long-term rentals and properties for sale. The project involved designing and developing a professional website that brings all property services together on one accessible platform. Its purpose was to help customers discover available properties, view essential information, make booking enquiries through WhatsApp and access helpful real estate content.

Challenge

The client needed a central platform for presenting different property categories while keeping enquiries and bookings simple. The website also required an efficient back-office system where administrators could manage listings, monitor bookings, generate customer receipts and publish blog content without depending on a developer.

Solution

A responsive property website was developed with dedicated sections for Airbnb units, rental properties and properties for sale. Each listing presents property images, descriptions, pricing and important features in a clear format. WhatsApp booking was integrated to allow customers to contact the business directly. An administrative dashboard was also created for managing property listings, bookings, receipts and blog articles from one secure location.

Final Result

The completed platform provides Pelek Properties with a professional digital presence and a streamlined system for promoting and managing its properties. Visitors can easily explore available listings and begin bookings through WhatsApp, while administrators can update properties, manage booking records, generate receipts and publish blog content through the website’s dashboard.
